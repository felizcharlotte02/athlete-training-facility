The training flow

Every session has a purpose.

01. Warm up with intention.Prepare for the planned session with movement appropriate to the day's activities.
02. Focus on technique.Practice movements with coaching and attention to control, form, and individual readiness.
03. Build the work capacity.Complete planned strength, movement, or conditioning exercises at an appropriate level.
04. Review and recover.Finish with suitable recovery activities and review the session's priorities.
Train for the full season

Preparation doesn't stop at practice.

Offseason

Establish consistency, revisit movement fundamentals, and work toward development goals.

In Season

Coordinate training with practices, competition, and recovery needs for a balanced schedule.

Between Seasons

Review progress, set fresh priorities, and plan a gradual transition into the next training phase.

Training should reflect each athlete's experience, readiness, and sport schedule. Youth athletes and experienced competitors need different levels of instruction, intensity, and progression.
