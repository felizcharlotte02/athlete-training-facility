The Training Framework

Four steps toward lasting progress.

A structured approach helps athletes understand their priorities and work toward improvement one session at a time.

STEP 01

Establish

Set goals, review experience, and focus on foundational technique.

STEP 02

Develop

Build strength and movement competency through consistent practice.

STEP 03

Progress

Adjust training demands as technique, confidence, and capacity improve.

STEP 04

Evaluate

Review progress and identify priorities for the next training phase.

Train With Intention

It's not just about lifting more. It's about getting better.

An effective training plan considers the athlete as a whole. Exercise selection, training volume, technique, and recovery all contribute to a thoughtful approach to development.

Individual goals

Build a plan around current needs and longer-term objectives.

Quality technique

Prioritize controlled, appropriate movement before adding difficulty.

Progressive training

Make changes gradually as the athlete develops.

Consistent effort

Focus on repeatable sessions instead of expecting overnight results.
