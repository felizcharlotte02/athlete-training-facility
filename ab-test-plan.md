A/B Test Plan – Hawkeye Performance
1. Page and Element Being Tested
This experiment will test the main headline on the Hawkeye Performance homepage. The headline is one of the first things visitors see when they arrive at the website. The goal is to determine which headline encourages more visitors to explore the website and its training options.
2. Version A and Version B
Version A (Original):
BUILD YOUR GAME. ELEVATE YOUR PERFORMANCE.
Version B (New):
YOUR NEXT LEVEL STARTS HERE.
The only difference between the two versions will be the main homepage headline. All other website content, images, colors, buttons, supporting text, and layout will remain exactly the same.
3. Hypothesis
I believe Version B will encourage more visitors to explore the available training options because it creates curiosity and makes visitors want to learn more about what Hawkeye Performance offers. Version A focuses more directly on athletic development and performance, while Version B uses a shorter and more motivating message that could catch a visitor's attention.
4. AI Visitor Persona and Testing Prompt
Each trial will use a fresh AI conversation with no memory of previous trials.
AI Model: ChatGPT GPT-6. The same model and settings will be used for all trials.
Visitor Persona: A high school athlete living in the Iowa City area who participates in school sports and is interested in improving athletic performance outside regular team practices.
Exact Testing Prompt:
"You are a high school athlete living in the Iowa City area. You participate in school sports and are interested in improving your athletic performance outside of regular team practices. You are considering finding a local training facility to help you improve. Review the following website homepage as if you were a potential customer. Based on the information provided, would you click one of the calls to action to explore the available training options? Answer YES or NO, followed by one sentence explaining your decision.
The same prompt and visitor persona will be used for every trial. Only the homepage headline will change between versions.
5. Assignment and Randomization
The experiment will include 100 total AI visitor trials. Fifty visitors will be randomly assigned to Version A and fifty visitors will be randomly assigned to Version B.
Before testing begins, a randomized list of all 100 assignments will be created. Each trial will use a fresh conversation, and the assigned version will be recorded to keep the experiment organized and consistent.
6. Primary Outcome
The primary outcome will be whether the AI visitor says they would click one of the calls to action to explore the available training options.
Responses will be recorded as:
•	YES = 1
•	NO = 0
The percentage of YES responses will be calculated for each version.
7. Sample Size and Timeline
The experiment will include 50 trials for Version A and 50 trials for Version B, for a total of 100 trials.
The experiment plan will be committed to GitHub before November 9, 2026. Data collection and analysis will be completed by November 15, 2026.
8. Analysis
The YES response rate for each version will be calculated by dividing the number of YES responses by 50. The difference between the two response rates will be compared to determine which headline performed better.
A two-proportion statistical test will be used to evaluate whether the difference is statistically significant. If the expected counts are too small, Fisher's exact test will be considered.
The hypothesis will be supported if Version B has a higher YES response rate and the difference is statistically significant. If there is no statistically significant difference, that result will be reported honestly.
9. Limitations
Because this experiment uses AI visitors instead of real customers, the results may not fully represent actual customer behavior. AI responses can help evaluate the clarity and appeal of different headlines, but they cannot perfectly predict real website clicks, purchases, or customer decisions.
