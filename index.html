Welcome to HPC

One facility. More ways to get better.

Finding reliable indoor practice space can be a challenge for athletes and teams, especially when Iowa weather changes the plan. Hawkeye Performance Center is designed to make training more convenient by combining open practice areas, performance coaching, and conditioning opportunities in one accessible location.

We welcome developing youth athletes, high school competitors, college athletes, recreational teams, and coaches looking for a consistent place to work toward their goals.

Our approach

Progress is a team effort.

Our fictional coaching team, led by performance director Jordan Ellis, believes great training starts with clear goals, consistent effort, and a supportive environment. Sessions are designed around the athlete or team's experience, priorities, and schedule.

  • Training plans shaped around individual and team goals
  • Indoor space for practices, drills, and athletic development
  • Options for individuals, small groups, and full teams
  • A welcoming environment for different ages and experience levels
