Train the athlete. Elevate the game.

Sports performance training focuses on movement skills that matter across sports, helping athletes practice with purpose and develop at a pace that fits their experience. Fictional performance director Jordan Ellis leads the coaching concept with an emphasis on technique, consistency, and measurable goals.

01 / EXPLODE

Speed + Acceleration

Practice starting mechanics, running form, and controlled acceleration to support faster, more efficient movement.

02 / REACT

Agility + Direction

Build body control, deceleration, balance, and change-of-direction skills through guided movement drills.

03 / CONTROL

Coordination + Power

Develop timing, body awareness, and explosive movement through appropriate drills and coached progressions.
