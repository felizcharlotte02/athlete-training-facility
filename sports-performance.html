Sports Performance Training · Iowa City

Train the qualities that make you a better athlete.

Hawkeye Performance Center brings sports performance training to a convenient indoor setting in Iowa City. Our fictional program focuses on athletic movement, speed, agility, coordination, balance, and power for athletes at different stages of development.

Whether you're preparing for a season, building a foundation, or working toward a new personal goal, training begins with understanding what you want to improve.

The performance difference

Build skills that transfer to competition.

Sports performance training develops the physical qualities athletes use across multiple sports. Rather than focusing on one sport's techniques, sessions emphasize movement skills that support performance on the field, court, track, or other playing surface.

Fictional performance director Jordan Ellis and the coaching team create structured sessions that match each participant's age, training experience, and goals. The focus is on learning sound movement patterns, practicing consistently, and progressing at an appropriate pace.

Speed & Acceleration

Practice starting mechanics, efficient movement, and controlled acceleration to support faster, more confident play.

Agility & Change of Direction

Use planned movement drills to practice body control, balance, deceleration, and directional changes.

Coordination & Power

Develop timing, body awareness, and explosive movement through age-appropriate drills and coached progressions.

Training options

Find the right fit for your goals.

Individual Performance Sessions

One-on-one coaching with time to focus on personal goals, movement technique, and areas for improvement.

Ideal for: Athletes who prefer individualized feedback and a focused training plan.

Small-Group Training

Train alongside a few other athletes through guided drills that encourage effort, consistency, and friendly competition.

Ideal for: Friends, teammates, and athletes who enjoy learning together.

Team Performance Sessions

Bring a team together for coordinated warm-ups, movement drills, and performance-focused practice blocks.

Ideal for: Coaches planning preseason development or in-season training.

Your training journey

A clear process from day one.

  • Set a goal: Identify the movement skills or physical qualities you want to develop.
  • Establish a starting point: Discuss experience, activity level, and training priorities with a coach.
  • Train with purpose: Follow structured drills with clear instruction and feedback.
  • Review your progress: Revisit your goals and adjust the training focus as you develop.

Designed for athletes at every stage

Youth participants can focus on movement fundamentals and confidence. High school and college athletes can work on performance goals that complement their sports schedules. Recreational athletes can build general athletic ability and enjoy a structured challenge.

Keep building

Connect performance with your sport.

Once you've identified your broader performance goals, explore sport-specific training for drills tailored to your game or strength and conditioning for a more structured approach to physical development.
